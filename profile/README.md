**Fill PDF forms, generate PDFs and Word documents, and collect e-signatures with one API.**

Upload a PDF form or a Word document, design a page, or write HTML. Send JSON from your app and get back a finished
PDF or Word file, ready to sign. [Start free](https://docspring.com/sign_up): 200 credits every month and unlimited test
PDFs, no credit card needed.

### AI assistants

- **MCP server:** `https://mcp.docspring.com/mcp` (US) or `https://mcp-eu.docspring.com/mcp` (EU). Connect Claude,
  ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code or Codex and sign in: [setup guide](https://docspring.com/docs/mcp/).
- **Agent Skills:** [DocSpring/skills](https://github.com/DocSpring/skills) teaches coding agents the API.
  `npx skills add DocSpring/skills`
